Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 20, 2026

Student Launcher is developed by thats.jainam.

No data leaves your device. Student Launcher has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network access. We do not collect, transmit or share personal data with us or anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings, widget layout, tasks, pinned apps and marked apps (stored locally in the app's private storage).
  • Your chosen wallpaper photo(s), copied into the app's private storage when you pick them with the system photo picker.

This data is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. Device backup for the app is disabled (allowBackup is off), so nothing is copied to cloud backup.

Retention and deletion

The data above stays on your device only as long as you keep the app. To delete it, clear the app's storage in system settings (Settings → Apps → Student Launcher → Storage → Clear storage) or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling removes all settings, tasks, layouts and wallpaper copies. We never receive deletion requests because we never receive your data.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): used only to show focus reminders while you are in an app you marked as distracting. You can turn this off at any time in the app or in system settings.

Installed apps

The app reads the list of installed apps on your device only to show your home screen and app list. This list stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

Photos

Wallpaper photos are chosen with Android's system photo picker. The app does not read your full gallery; it only receives the photos you select, and only stores a copy in its private storage for the wallpaper.

Third-party widgets

Android widgets you add are provided by their own apps and follow their own privacy policies.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact thats.jainam on GitHub.
